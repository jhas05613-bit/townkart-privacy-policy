Privacy Policy

This policy explains how TownKart handles information when you use our shopping, seller and delivery services.

TownKart (“TownKart”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is a local marketplace application intended to connect customers with shops and delivery partners. This Privacy Policy describes how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you use TownKart.

1. About TownKart

TownKart supports three main roles: Customers, Shop Owners (Merchants), and Delivery Partners (Riders). The information processed depends on the features you use and your role. We aim to use information only for legitimate marketplace, order fulfilment, security, support and service-improvement purposes.

2. Information we handle

Account and profile information

Depending on how you register, we may process your name, email address, mobile number, Firebase Authentication user identifier, account role, account status and other profile details you choose to provide. Google Sign-In, email/password sign-in or phone verification may be available according to the app configuration. We do not intend to store raw passwords in the TownKart Firestore database.

Customer, address and order information

To support shopping and delivery, TownKart may process saved addresses, recipient details, locality or ward, district, postal code, landmarks, delivery instructions, cart items, products ordered, quantities, order status, cancellation details, order IDs and customer support requests. Provide only the address information needed to complete delivery.

Shop Owner information

When applying to operate a shop, you may provide a shop name, owner and contact details, shop category, service area, business address, product listings, prices and inventory. If the registration form requests them, we may also process business verification details such as GSTIN or FSSAI licence information and related verification status.

Rider information

When applying as a Delivery Partner, you may provide your name, mobile number, ward or service area, vehicle type, vehicle registration number and driving licence details where requested. We may also process application status, assigned deliveries, delivery history and relevant earnings or payout records.

Device, diagnostics and service information

Our configured service providers may process technical information necessary to authenticate accounts, deliver the service, secure the platform or diagnose errors. Depending on what is enabled, this may include app and operating-system version, authentication/session identifiers, crash or diagnostic information, and notification registration tokens.

3. How we use information

  • Create and manage user accounts and role-based access.
  • Display products, manage carts, create orders and show order history.
  • Allow shops to prepare orders and riders to fulfil authorised deliveries.
  • Verify shop and rider applications where applicable.
  • Manage delivery addresses, order status, handover verification and customer support.
  • Maintain marketplace security, detect misuse and troubleshoot service failures.
  • Send transactional or promotional notifications only if the relevant feature is implemented and enabled.
  • Meet applicable legal, accounting and dispute-resolution obligations.

4. How information may be shared

For an order: the shop receives the information needed to prepare and fulfil the order. The assigned rider may receive the delivery destination and relevant customer contact details. The customer may receive relevant shop and delivery information. Access should be limited to the people involved in the particular order.

Service providers: TownKart uses Firebase services for configured authentication, database and backend functions. Google services may be involved when Google Sign-In is used. If map, geocoding, routing, diagnostics or push-notification providers are enabled, those services may receive the technical data needed to provide the feature. Their own terms and privacy policies may apply.

Legal and safety reasons: information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect users, investigate fraud, or protect the security of TownKart.

TownKart does not intend to sell personal information to third parties for their independent marketing. We do not disclose more information than is reasonably needed for the stated purpose, subject to applicable law and the actual service configuration.

5. Location and maps

Manual address entry may be available without device-location permission. If a version of TownKart includes a current-location, map-pin or live-delivery-tracking feature, Android may ask for foreground location permission when you use that feature. If permission is granted, the app may process actual device coordinates for address selection or delivery operations.

If live Rider tracking is enabled, an assigned Rider’s location may be processed during an eligible active delivery to show delivery progress. Location access should be limited to the relevant delivery purpose and restricted to authorised participants. Background location, if ever used, requires separate implementation, appropriate disclosures and applicable Android permissions.

If location permission is denied, some location-based features may be unavailable; manual address entry may still be offered. This section must be updated if location or map services are not present in the released app or if their behaviour changes.

6. Notifications

If push notifications are enabled, TownKart may use Firebase Cloud Messaging or another configured service to send relevant order, account, delivery or promotional messages. Delivery depends on device connectivity, app configuration, operating-system settings and notification permission. You can manage notification permission through Android Settings. Do not rely on a push notification as the only record of an order or transaction.

7. Orders and payment information

When Cash on Delivery (COD) is available, TownKart may process order totals, applicable fees, order status and COD collection status needed to fulfil and reconcile the order. An order record alone is not proof that cash was collected. TownKart does not claim to operate a payment gateway unless one is actually integrated in the released version.

8. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for account operation, order fulfilment, security, customer support, disputes and applicable legal or accounting requirements. Profile and address information may be retained until updated or deleted. Certain order or transaction records may need to be retained for lawful recordkeeping, fraud prevention or dispute resolution even after an account deletion request. Unnecessary location history should not be retained indefinitely.

9. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your TownKart account and associated personal data using the external deletion-request page below. The request may require reasonable verification that you control the account. We will process a valid request and delete or de-identify data that is no longer needed, subject to lawful retention requirements. If some records must be retained for legal, accounting, security or dispute reasons, they will be restricted to those purposes.

Request account deletion: TownKart account and data deletion instructions.

Important: Before release, ensure the app also provides an in-app path to initiate account deletion and that the external deletion page works. Publishing this text alone does not implement deletion in the backend.

10. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures intended to protect information, including authentication, access controls and backend validation where implemented. Access to personal information should be limited to authorised users and services. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children’s privacy

TownKart is not intended to encourage children to independently submit personal, business or identity-verification information. Users must meet the age and legal eligibility requirements applicable to the services they use. If you believe information has been collected unlawfully from a child, contact us using the details below.

12. Your choices and requests

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You can update profile information and addresses through available app controls, manage Android permissions in device Settings, and request account deletion through the process described above. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in TownKart, its service providers, data practices or legal obligations. We will update the “Last updated” date when we publish a revision. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
